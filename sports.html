Pro Sports 🎾

Despite growing up in New York, I am a Boston Celtics 🍀 fan. My favorite color as a kid was green, so I first became a Michigan State Spartans fan in the days of Tom Izzo and Draymond Green. My first favorite NBA player was Rajon Rondo, after watching him play on after Dwyane Wade broke his arm in the 2011 Eastern Conference Semifinals. My Celtics fandom solidified after the Celtics drafted Jaylen Brown out of my alma mater Cal.

My favorite tennis player (and athlete) is Novak Djokovic. I admire his work ethic and mental toughness. The greatest tennis match I've watched is the 2019 Wimbledon Final, when Djokovic took down Federer in a fifth-set tiebreak. Now that Djokovic is close to retirement, I enjoy cheering on Carlos Alcaraz. I was lucky enough to get to meet Carlos in Monte Carlo in 2024 on one of the practice days before the tournament. Very nice guy!

I also like following track and field and distance running.
